## Library Card

A library card is free for all residents of Greenfield City and surrounding Greenfield County. Non-residents may obtain a card for an annual fee of $40. To register, bring a valid government-issued photo ID and proof of current address (utility bill, lease agreement, etc.).

- Cards expire every 3 years and can be renewed in person or online.
- Children under 14 must have a parent or guardian co-sign their library card application.
- Each card allows up to 50 items to be checked out at one time.

## Meeting Rooms

The library has three meeting rooms available for community use:
- **Room A (Maple Room):** Seats 12, includes whiteboard and TV with HDMI input. Free for non-profit use; $25/hour for private or commercial use.
- **Room B (Oak Room):** Seats 40, includes projector, screen, and microphone system. Free for non-profit use; $50/hour for private or commercial use.
- **Room C (Study Pod):** Seats 4, sound-dampened for quiet study. Free for all — first come, first served (2-hour limit).

Rooms may be reserved up to 30 days in advance via the library website or by calling the front desk at (555) 234-5678.

## Programs (2024–2025 Season)

### For Children (ages 0–12)
- **Story Time:** Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:30 AM (ages 0–5). Free, no registration required.
- **LEGO Club:** First Saturday of each month, 2:00 PM – 3:30 PM (ages 6–12). Free, registration required (limited to 20 participants).
- **Summer Reading Challenge:** June 1 – August 15. Read 20 books to earn a certificate and prize pack. Open to all ages 0–12.

### For Teens (ages 13–18)
- **Teen Advisory Board:** Meets the 2nd Wednesday of each month at 4:00 PM. Teens help plan library events and recommend purchases.
- **Homework Help Lab:** Monday – Thursday, 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM. Free tutoring by volunteer educators.
- **Gaming Night:** Last Friday of each month, 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM. Nintendo Switch, board games, and snacks provided.

### For Adults
- **Book Club:** Meets the 3rd Monday of each month at 6:30 PM. Current selection: "Demon Copperhead" by Barbara Kingsolver.
- **Digital Literacy Classes:** Wednesdays at 11:00 AM. Topics rotate weekly (email basics, online safety, smartphone tips, social media). Free, open to all.
- **ESL Conversation Circle:** Fridays at 10:00 AM. Practice English conversation in a supportive group setting. Free, no registration required.
- **Tax Preparation Assistance (VITA):** February 1 – April 15. Free tax prep for households earning under $64,000. By appointment only.

## Technology Services

- **Public Computers:** 24 desktops with internet access. Sessions limited to 60 minutes when others are waiting.
- **Wi-Fi:** Free, no password required. Network name: Greenfield_Library_WiFi
- **Printing & Copying:** B&W: $0.10/page; Color: $0.50/page. Scanning: Free.
- **3D Printing:** Available by appointment. Cost: $0.10 per gram of filament. Maximum print size: 8" × 8" × 8".
- **Mobile Hotspot Lending:** 10 hotspots available for checkout (7-day loan, one per household).
