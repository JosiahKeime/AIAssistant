# Pinnacle Ventures — Q3 2024 Earnings Report

## Executive Summary

Pinnacle Ventures Inc. (Ticker: PNVT) reported strong third-quarter results for the period ending September 30, 2024. Total revenue reached $847.3 million, representing a 12.4% year-over-year increase from $753.8 million in Q3 2023. Net income was $126.2 million, up from $98.7 million in the prior-year quarter, reflecting a net margin of 14.9%.

Adjusted EBITDA for Q3 2024 was $198.5 million, compared to $167.1 million in Q3 2023, an improvement of 18.8%.

## Revenue Breakdown by Segment

| Segment | Q3 2024 Revenue | Q3 2023 Revenue | YoY Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloud Services | $412.6M | $338.1M | +22.0% |
| Enterprise Software | $287.4M | $278.9M | +3.1% |
| Professional Services | $147.3M | $136.8M | +7.7% |
| **Total** | **$847.3M** | **$753.8M** | **+12.4%** |

Cloud Services continued to be the primary growth driver, accounting for 48.7% of total revenue. The segment benefited from strong adoption of the Pinnacle CloudOS platform, which added 1,240 net new enterprise customers during the quarter.

## Key Metrics

- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** $3.28 billion (up from $2.85 billion, +15.1% YoY)
- **Net Revenue Retention Rate:** 118% (vs. 114% in Q3 2023)
- **Gross Margin:** 68.3% (vs. 66.1% in Q3 2023)
- **Free Cash Flow:** $164.7 million (vs. $131.2 million in Q3 2023)
- **Cash and Equivalents:** $1.92 billion as of September 30, 2024
- **Total Debt:** $750 million (5-year senior unsecured notes at 4.25%, maturing June 2028)
- **Headcount:** 11,350 employees (vs. 10,200 in Q3 2023)

## Guidance for Q4 2024

Management issued the following guidance for Q4 2024:
- Revenue: $880M – $910M (consensus was $875M)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 23% – 24%
- Full-year 2024 revenue: $3.28B – $3.31B (raised from prior guidance of $3.20B – $3.25B)

CEO Robert Chang stated: "Our cloud transition is accelerating faster than expected. We're seeing particular strength in the financial services and healthcare verticals, where customers are increasingly choosing Pinnacle CloudOS over legacy on-premises solutions."

## Capital Allocation

- **Share Repurchase:** The board authorized an additional $500 million buyback program in August 2024, bringing total remaining authorization to $750 million.
- **Dividend:** Quarterly dividend of $0.18 per share, payable November 15, 2024. Annualized yield approximately 0.9% at current prices.
- **Acquisitions:** Completed the acquisition of DataStream Analytics for $215 million in July 2024. DataStream contributed approximately $12 million in revenue during Q3.

## Risk Factors

Key risks highlighted in the report:
1. Macroeconomic uncertainty could slow enterprise IT spending decisions
2. Increased competition from hyperscale cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP)
3. Foreign exchange headwinds — approximately 28% of revenue is denominated in non-USD currencies
4. Customer concentration: top 10 customers represent 18% of ARR
5. Ongoing cybersecurity threats and potential data breach liability
