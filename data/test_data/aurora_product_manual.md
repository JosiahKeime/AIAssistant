## Installation

### Step 1: Turn Off HVAC Power
Switch off power at the circuit breaker for your heating/cooling system. Verify power is off by checking that your existing thermostat display is blank.

### Step 2: Remove Old Thermostat
Take a photo of your existing wiring for reference. Label each wire using the provided sticker sheet. Disconnect wires one at a time and remove the old mounting plate.

### Step 3: Install Mounting Plate
Hold the Aurora mounting plate against the wall. Use a level to ensure it is straight. Mark drill holes and attach with the provided screws.

### Step 4: Connect Wires
Connect labeled wires to the corresponding terminals on the mounting plate:
- **Rh** — Red (24V heating)
- **Rc** — Red (24V cooling) — may be jumpered to Rh
- **W1** — White (heating stage 1)
- **Y1** — Yellow (cooling stage 1)
- **G** — Green (fan)
- **C** — Blue or brown (common)

### Step 5: Attach Thermostat
Snap the Aurora X-7 unit onto the mounting plate. Restore power at the circuit breaker.

## Smart Features

### Learning Schedule
The Aurora X-7 learns your temperature preferences over the first 7 days of use. After the learning period, it creates an automatic schedule. You can override or manually adjust the schedule at any time through the app or touchscreen.

### Geofencing
When enabled, the thermostat uses your phone's location to detect when you leave or arrive home. The geofence radius is configurable from 1 to 10 miles (default: 3 miles).

### Energy Reports
Monthly energy reports are available in the Aurora Home app. Reports include estimated energy savings compared to a fixed 72°F schedule, daily usage charts, and HVAC runtime hours.

### Voice Assistants
Compatible with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit. Setup requires linking your Aurora account in the respective voice assistant app.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Display is blank | Check circuit breaker. Verify C-wire connection. Try USB-C backup power. |
| Wi-Fi won't connect | Aurora X-7 only supports 2.4 GHz networks. Ensure your router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz SSID. |
| Temperature reading seems off | Place a separate thermometer next to the unit. Allow 15 minutes to stabilize. If difference exceeds 3°F, recalibrate via Settings > Sensors > Temperature Offset. |
| HVAC short cycling | Check for incorrect wiring. Ensure Rh and Rc jumper is set correctly for your system. |
| "No C-wire detected" error | Install the Aurora C-Wire Adapter (CWA-200) or use USB-C backup power. |
