# Ironwood Trail Marathon — Race Information & Runner's Guide

## Event Overview

The Ironwood Trail Marathon is an annual trail running event held in the Ironwood State Forest, located 45 miles northeast of Denver, Colorado. The race has been held every year since 2016 and attracts approximately 2,500 runners annually.

**2025 Race Date:** Saturday, June 14, 2025  
**Race Start Time:** 6:00 AM (all distances)  
**Location:** Ironwood State Forest Trailhead, 14200 Ironwood Canyon Road, Idaho Springs, CO 80452

## Course Description

All courses follow marked single-track and fire-road trails through the Ironwood State Forest. The terrain includes rocky mountain trails, creek crossings, alpine meadows, and forested switchbacks. Trail elevation ranges from 7,200 feet to 11,450 feet above sea level.

### Highlights
- **Mile 3 (all courses):** Cascade Falls viewpoint — a 120-foot waterfall visible from the trail
- **Mile 8 (Half Marathon +):** Ridge Line section with panoramic views of the Continental Divide
- **Mile 18 (Marathon + 50K):** The "Grinder" — a 1,400-foot climb over 2.5 miles, the toughest segment of the course
- **Mile 26 (50K only):** Alpine Lake turnaround at 11,450 feet elevation

All aid stations provide: water, electrolyte drink (Skratch Labs), bananas, oranges, PB&J sandwiches, pretzels, and gummy bears. Stations 4, 5, and 7 also offer hot broth and boiled potatoes.

**Drop bags:** Marathon and 50K runners may place one drop bag at Station 4 (Mile 15). Drop bags must be labeled with your bib number and submitted at check-in.

## Mandatory Gear (50K and Marathon only)

All marathon and 50K runners must carry the following at all times during the race:
- Minimum 1.5 liters of water/hydration capacity
- Emergency whistle
- Emergency blanket (space blanket)
- Fully charged cell phone
- Headlamp with fresh batteries (50K only)

Gear checks will be conducted at the start line. Runners who do not have mandatory gear will not be permitted to start.

## Awards

- Top 3 male and female finishers in each distance receive handcrafted wooden trophies made from reclaimed ironwood trees
- All finishers receive a commemorative medal and finisher's t-shirt
- Age group awards (5-year brackets) for top 3 in each bracket
- The "Ironwood Legend" award goes to any runner who completes all four distances across four consecutive years

## Safety & Rules

1. Stay on the marked course at all times. Blue-and-white trail markers are placed every 0.25 miles.
2. Yield to uphill runners and all equestrian trail users.
3. No earbuds or headphones are permitted on the 50K course for safety. Bone conduction headphones are allowed for all other distances.
4. No pacers are allowed for any distance.
5. No dogs, strollers, or wheeled devices on the course.
6. Littering results in immediate disqualification. Pack out all trash.
7. If you drop out mid-race, you must notify the nearest aid station volunteer and turn in your bib.
